Research

I study what social media feeds show people about health and the brain, and what people actually watch. My work falls into four themes.

What TikTok shows, and what people watch

Donated TikTok histories linked to surveys: health content and misinformation, served and watched.

The brain in public

How the brain, ADHD and neurotechnology are framed online, and what neuroethics should learn from it.

Radicalization and misinformation

How movements build narratives, from Querdenken during COVID-19 to radical content in young adults' feeds.

Language models and medical ethics

Language models in informed consent, and how AI chatbots answer questions about contested history across languages.
